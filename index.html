Treatments we offer

From a routine cleaning to implants, all under one roof on 100 Feet Road.

  • Teeth cleaning

    Scaling and polishing to remove tartar and stains.

  • Teeth whitening

    In-clinic whitening to lighten your natural shade.

  • Veneers

    Thin porcelain shells for chipped, stained or uneven front teeth.

  • Dental implants

    A titanium post and crown to replace a missing tooth.

  • Dental crowns

    A custom cap that protects and restores a weak or broken tooth.

  • Braces & aligners

    Metal or ceramic braces, and clear aligners.

  • Root canal treatment

    Saves an infected tooth by cleaning out the inside.

  • Tooth fillings

    Tooth-coloured fillings for cavities and chipped teeth.

  • Dentures & bridges

    Full and partial dentures, and fixed bridges.

  • Wisdom tooth removal

    Gentle removal of painful or stuck wisdom teeth.

  • Kids’ dentistry

    Friendly check-ups and care for children’s milk teeth.

  • Dental emergencies

    Toothache, swelling or a broken tooth? Message us straight away.

Your first visit

  1. Send us a WhatsApp message or walk in, and we'll fix a time.

  2. Tell your dentist about any pain, past treatment and medicines you take.

  3. Your teeth and gums are checked, with X-rays if needed, and we show you what we see.

  4. You get your options and their cost in writing, and decide what you want done.
