What we collect

Only what we need to book your visits and look after your teeth:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email, from a call, WhatsApp, email or the front desk.
  • Booking details: what you're coming in for and when suits you.
  • Health information: your medical and dental history, X-rays, scans, photos and treatment notes. We only collect these during your care.
  • Payment details: invoices, insurance details and instalment plans. Card payments go through our payment provider, and we never store your full card number.
  • Website data: basic information such as your browser and the pages you visited, collected through cookies (see Cookies).

How we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you about appointments.
  • To examine you, plan your treatment and carry it out safely.
  • To prepare quotes, bills and insurance or instalment paperwork.
  • To send aftercare instructions and check-up reminders.
  • To keep the records that the law and our professional rules require.

We will never sell your information to anyone.

Your dental records

Your records are confidential. Only the dentists and staff involved in your care can see them. We'll only use before-and-after photos on our website or social media if you give us separate written permission, and you can take that back at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it:

  • The dental lab that makes your crown, aligners or dentures, and any specialist we refer you to.
  • Your insurer or instalment provider, when you ask us to.
  • The companies behind our booking software, messaging and secure storage, who are bound to keep it confidential.
  • Government or legal authorities, but only if the law requires it.

Cookies

Our website uses a few cookies to work properly, and some optional ones that tell us which pages people find useful. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site might not work as well.

Calls & messages

When you ask us for an appointment, you're agreeing that we can call, text, WhatsApp or email you about your appointment and care. We'll only send offers or news if you've said yes to them. You can stop these at any time by replying "STOP" or telling us at the desk.

How long we keep it

We keep dental records for as long as medical record-keeping rules require. If you got in touch but never booked a visit, we delete your details within [12 months].

Keeping it safe

We use encrypted software, limit who can see what, back everything up securely, and train our team on privacy. No system is perfect, but if something ever goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you quickly.

Your rights

Under Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Show you, or give you a copy of, the information we hold about you, including your dental records.
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date.
  • Stop sending you marketing, or stop using your photos.
  • Delete information we aren't legally required to keep.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (details below).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
